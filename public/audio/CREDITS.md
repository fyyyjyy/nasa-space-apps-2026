## Caveats

- Mood fit was chosen from each page's description and tags. The tracks were not listened to. In particular, no CC0 track was found that clearly "builds from tense to triumphant" (Apollo 11) or is "adventurous with a bittersweet resolution" (Apollo 17). Review by ear before release.
- `space-ambient.m4a` and `apollo11.m4a` and `insight.m4a` come from sources not labelled as pre-looped by the author. The Dark Sci-Fi pack is described as loopable, and the author recommends a slight crossfade at the loop point. `steller_dreams` has no loop version.
- AAC adds a small encoder delay, so loops may have a very short gap.
